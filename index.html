System Architecture

How SmartPollution AI Operates

End-to-end integration of edge IoT hardware, atmospheric neural forecasting, and municipal automated mitigation.

Step 1

IoT Edge Sensing

Multi-spectral laser particulate counters and electrochemical sensors capture PM2.5, PM10, CO₂, NO₂, and O₃ every 3 seconds.

Step 2

Telemetry Ingestion

Data streams undergo automated outlier filtering, Kalman smoothing, and temperature-humidity baseline recalibration.

Step 3

Bi-LSTM Neural Engine

Recurrent deep learning models analyze meteorological winds, traffic influx, and historical cycles to forecast future AQI spikes.

Step 4

Actionable Response

Instant dispatch of smart traffic signal adjustments, industrial particulate misting cannons, and public citizen health advisories.
